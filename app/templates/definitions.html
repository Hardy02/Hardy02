Stat Definitions

Back to dashboard
  • Hands: Number of hands where the selected player was dealt in.
  • VPIP: Percentage of hands where the player voluntarily put money in preflop (calls, bets, raises). Forced blinds/antes do not count.
  • PFR: Percentage of hands where the player raised preflop.
  • 3B: Percentage of opportunities where the player re-raised preflop after facing exactly one open raise.
  • Fv3B: Percentage of opportunities where the player opened preflop, faced a 3-bet, and then folded preflop.
  • ST (Steal): Percentage of opportunities where the player opened first-in from CO, BTN, or SB.
  • FvST: Percentage of opportunities where the player was in the blinds and folded to a steal attempt from CO, BTN, or SB.
  • CBET: Percentage of opportunities where the preflop aggressor bet or raised on the flop.
  • FvCBET: Percentage of opportunities where the player faced a flop continuation bet and folded.
  • WTSD: Percentage of hands where the player went to showdown out of hands where they saw the flop.
  • WSD: Percentage of showdowns won out of showdowns played.
